🧱 bricktrait - Build Your Own LEGO Avatar

bricktrait is a free LEGO® minifigure avatar maker that runs entirely in your browser. You pick from more than 3,000 real LDraw parts, put them together into a minifig, and export the finished result as a PNG you can use as a profile picture anywhere.

✨ What Is bricktrait?

bricktrait is a character creator built for people who love LEGO and want a profile picture that stands out. Instead of drawing an avatar by hand or paying for a subscription service, you assemble your minifig from authentic parts and download the finished image in a few clicks.

The application runs in a normal web browser. That means there is nothing complicated to configure. You open it, you build, you save. The parts you choose from are not generic cartoon shapes. They come from the LDraw library, which is the long-running community standard for LEGO part geometry. Every head, torso, hair piece, hat, and accessory is modelled from a genuine part.

Because everything happens on your own computer, your avatar is yours. There is no account to create, no upload queue, and no watermark stamped across the final PNG.

🎨 What You Can Do With It

  • Pick from 3,000+ real parts. Heads, torsos, legs, hair, helmets, hats, and accessories taken from the LDraw part library.
  • Build a full minifig. Combine parts into a complete figure, then rotate and inspect it before you commit.
  • See it in 3D. The browser renders your character live, so you always know exactly how it will look.
  • Export a clean PNG. Save your creation as a transparent or solid-background image sized for a profile picture.
  • Use it as a pfp anywhere. Discord, forums, social media, GitHub, game profiles, and chat apps all accept a standard PNG.
  • No account, no cost. The tool is free and opens straight in your browser.

💾 Download and Setup

All downloads are handled through the project page on GitHub. Visit this link to download the application:

Download bricktrait

Once the page opens in your browser, look for the green Code button near the top right of the file list. Click it, then choose Download ZIP. A small file will land in your Downloads folder.

Next, open your Downloads folder in File Explorer. Right-click the ZIP file you just saved, choose Extract All…, and confirm the destination folder. Windows will unpack the contents into a normal folder you can open.

After extraction, open the new folder and look for the project files inside. Everything you need is included in that folder. Because bricktrait runs in the browser, you do not go through a traditional installer wizard with "Next" buttons — the application files are the application.

When you are finished, you can delete the ZIP file. Keep the extracted folder somewhere sensible, such as your Documents folder, so you can find it again later.

🖱️ How to Make Your Avatar

  1. Open bricktrait. Launch the application so the builder loads in your browser window.
  2. Start with a head. Browse the catalogue and pick a face that fits the character you have in mind.
  3. Add the body. Choose a torso and legs. You can swap them as many times as you like before saving.
  4. Finish with hair or a hat. This is the step where most avatars become recognisable, so take your time here.
  5. Check the angle. Spin the model around. Look at it from the front and from a slight angle, the way a profile picture is usually seen.
  6. Export your PNG. Use the export option to save the image to your computer.
  7. Upload it anywhere. Open your chat app, forum, or social profile and set the PNG as your picture.

📋 What You Need

bricktrait is designed to be light. A typical modern Windows laptop or desktop will handle it comfortably. The checklist below is a realistic guide for smooth performance:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11. The instructions on this page assume a Windows computer.
  • A current web browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or any modern browser that supports 3D graphics.
  • A working graphics driver. Keeping your browser and graphics driver up to date gives the smoothest rotation and rendering.
  • Around 4 GB of memory. Enough to keep the browser and the 3D scene running at the same time.
  • A few hundred megabytes of free disk space. Used for the application files and the images you export.
  • An internet connection for the download. Once the files are on your computer, browsing and building are local activities.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Is bricktrait really free?
Yes. There is no paid tier, no trial period, and no subscription. You download it and use it.

Do I need to know how to code?
No. Downloading the files and opening the application requires only the standard Windows steps described above.

Will my avatar have a watermark?
No. The PNG you export is your own image and is not stamped with branding.

Can I use the avatar for commercial work?
The software is free to use, but LEGO is a trademark of the LEGO Group, and the LDraw part library has its own attribution terms. Check the licence files included in the folder before using an avatar commercially.

Where are my exported images saved?
Your browser saves them to the default download location, usually your Downloads folder.

What if the page will not open?
Confirm the ZIP file finished extracting, try a different modern browser, and make sure no security software is blocking local files.

🧰 Built With

bricktrait is built with Svelte and SvelteKit for the interface, Three.js and WebGL for the 3D rendering, and TypeScript throughout. The part geometry comes from LDraw. It is an open project, so you can look through the source, report a problem, or suggest an improvement on the project page.
